## One line

You have made it here. That makes you special already.

## Why I coach

For twenty-six years I have been the one in the room who had to decide. I built and ran businesses in Europe and in the US: a video product line for a billion-euro subscription business at Vodafone, the European operations and then the global direct-to-consumer business of a Silicon Valley start-up that grew from a $150M to a $2.6BN valuation while I was on its leadership team, and most recently a large consumer business in Switzerland with fifty people and several hundred thousand paying customers.

Somewhere along the way I noticed two things.

First: the hardest problems I ever faced were never really about strategy, money or technology. They were about a person — often me — being stuck in a story about what was (im)possible. Often the victim of factors or people beyond one's control. The moments that changed everything were conversations. A few of them, with the right person, at the right time.

Second: almost nobody at the top has that person. The higher you go, the fewer people will tell you the truth, and the fewer places you can think out loud without it costing you something.

I coach because I want to be that person for a small number of amazing people — and because I have found nothing more alive than watching someone see, in real time, that the wall they've been hitting against is vanishing.

## Who I work with

People who carry real responsibility and aim for ultra-high performance: CEOs, general managers, founders, executives running a P&L, a country, a product, a team of hundreds. Usually at an inflection point — a bigger role, a company that has outgrown its playbook, a decision they keep postponing, a success that somehow doesn't feel like one.

I work in English, German and French, with people anywhere in the world, from Zurich and online.

I take on very few clients at a time. Not as a marketing line — because the work is deeply human and precise. It's not software, it doesn't scale, and I don't want it to.

## The promise

You will make the decision you've been circling. You will stop running on a story about yourself that is ten years out of date. And you will do it faster and with more nerve than you would alone — because the person across from you has run the numbers, sat in the board meeting, made the payroll, and also knows that none of that is the real conversation when it comes to ultra-high performance.

I don't follow a standard process. I promise my full focus before, during and after our conversations, my honesty, and my experience — and that I will never tell you what you want to hear.

## How I work

Two registers, on purpose.

**The mentor.** I have owned P&Ls, scaled teams, launched products, and been through the growth of a start-up from series A to unicorn. I know what a board wants, what a bad hire costs, and how a go-to-market plan actually fails. When you need someone who has done it, you get that.

**The coach.** The mentor is not what unlocks people entirely. What unlocks people is being seen clearly, being asked the question nobody dares to ask, and being given room to answer it honestly.

Most coaches live at one end of this spectrum. I think the value is in holding both, and knowing which one the moment calls for.

## The deal

There is no standard proposal and no price list.

It starts with a conversation — a real one. A powerful 90-minute coaching conversation, no charge, no pitch. Not a "discovery call", not a "coffee chat". A coaching conversation, about the thing you're actually facing. Coming out of it, you and I both decide what's next: by the end of it you will know whether I can help you, and so will I.

If we both want to continue, I make you a proposal for the whole engagement — typically six to twelve months — and we agree a single fee for it. You are not buying hours. You are buying access to someone who is thinking about you and your situation between the sessions as well as during them.

I don't advertise. Everyone I work with came through someone I already know. If you're reading this, that's probably how you got here too.

## FAQs (the assistant answers from these)

**Are you taking on new clients?**
A few, yes. Christian keeps the number very small on purpose. The way in is a conversation, not an application — write to him.

**What does it cost?**
There is no standard price list. After a first conversation, if both sides want to continue, Christian proposes a single fee for the whole engagement, usually six to twelve months.

**What is the first conversation?**
About 90 minutes, no charge, no pitch. It is a real coaching experience about whatever you're actually facing — not a sales call or a coffee chat. By the end you will know whether this is for you, and Christian will know if he can and wants to help. Coming out of it, you both decide what's next.

**Is it therapy?**
No. Coaching is about where you're going and how to get there, less about healing where you've been. If what someone needs is therapy, Christian will say so, respectfully.

**Where are you based? Do you work remotely?**
Zurich. Clients globally. Online works perfectly well. In-person meetings can and will happen upon arrangement.

**Which languages?**
English, German, French — natively or fluently. Sessions can switch between them.

**Are you certified?**
Christian is training with the Co-Active Training Institute (ICF-accredited) from September 2026, working toward the ICF ACC credential. Before that: twenty-six years of running businesses and coaching team members, which is the part of the CV clients tend to care about.

**Do you coach teams or run workshops?**
The core work is one-to-one. Team work occasionally, by invitation, when it grows out of an existing engagement.

**Who are your role models?**
Coaches who work with few clients, and go deep: Rich Litvin and Steve Chandler, among others.

**Do you still have a day job?**
Christian has created his own coaching practice, to which he is dedicated 100%. It is his day job. Until recently he ran a large consumer business in Zurich; that is now a past chapter.

**Can I refer someone to you?**
Yes — that's how this works. Write to Christian with a line about the person and why you thought of them.

**How do I get in touch?**
By email: hello@christian-constant.com. There is no booking form on purpose — a few lines from you is the right first step.

**Do you have any coaching experience? Since when do you coach?**
Throughout his corporate and start-up career, it's the part of the job Christian has enjoyed the most: helping his co-workers see what they couldn't, and accepting feedback in return. Since 2026 he has decided to focus entirely on coaching and to create his own tailor-made coaching practice.

**What is Christian's coaching philosophy?**
Christian firmly believes that everyone has a lot of untapped or blocked potential. Everyone has goals and even dreams that they don't think are within reach when in fact they are much closer than they imagine. In his words: "Let's understand what might hold you back. And once we do, we will erase it methodically, like these interferences never existed in the first place."
